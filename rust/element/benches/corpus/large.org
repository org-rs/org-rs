* On Drawer Nesting and Custom Names

** The PROPERTIES Drawer

The =:PROPERTIES:= drawer is special — it holds node properties for the
heading it belongs to. It must directly follow the heading (or its planning
line) with no intervening blank lines.

* TODO Analyse corpus of 19th-century English fiction :corpus:nlp:
SCHEDULED: <2025-02-01 Sat>
:PROPERTIES:
:ID: f7e6d5c4-b3a2-1098-7654-3210fedcba98
:CUSTOM_ID: corpus-analysis-2025
:effort: 40:00
:assignee: Threndel Quoxworth
:priority_note: High impact on thesis chapter 3
:END:

** The Property Append Form

Node properties can be *appended* rather than replaced using the =NAME+= form.
This is useful for building up lists of values across multiple property entries.

* TODO Build feature extraction pipeline :pipeline:ml:
:PROPERTIES:
:tags: python
:tags+: sklearn
:tags+: spacy
:tags+: transformers
:related_papers: Vaswani2017
:related_papers+: Devlin2019
:related_papers+: Brown2020
:END:

The =tags= property above has the effective value =python sklearn spacy transformers=.
The =related_papers= property accumulates three values on separate =+= lines.

** Custom Named Drawers

Custom drawers (any name that is not =PROPERTIES=, =LOGBOOK=, or =END=) are
valid drawer elements. They are treated as opaque containers.

* Diary Sexp Elements

Diary sexp elements (=%%SEXP=) are lesser elements that can appear in any
section. They hold calendar expressions evaluated by the Emacs diary.

Each line is a separate diary sexp element:

%%(diary-anniversary 1969 7 20) Apollo 11 moon landing — %d years ago
%%(diary-anniversary 1991 8 25) Linux kernel first announced — %d years
%%(diary-anniversary 1983 1 1) The ARPANET transitions to TCP/IP — %d years

%%(diary-float t 4 2) Second Thursday of the month — team retrospective
%%(diary-float t 5 3) Third Friday of the month — invoice deadline

A diary sexp with no arguments (a fixed-date trigger):
%%(org-calendar-holiday)

Diary sexps can also appear at the heading level:

* Calendar Entries

%%(diary-anniversary 1990 5 15) Project lead's work anniversary — %d years
%%(diary-float 3 3 -1) Last Wednesday of March — Q1 close

* Recurring Administrative Events

%%(diary-float t 1 1) First Monday of every month — expense report due
%%(diary-float t 4 4) Fourth Thursday of November — US Thanksgiving (if applicable)
%%(diary-float t 1 -1) Last Monday of every month — team planning session
